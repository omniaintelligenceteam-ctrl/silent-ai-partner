## 2. How a deal happens today (reconstructed buyer journey)

**Remote buyer (their core customer — "California to New York" per their own site):**
1. Discovers a unit on YouTube walkthrough, Facebook/IG reel, RacingJunk, RV Trader, or the site. [V — all channels confirmed active]
2. Wants detail. The listing gives him **58–96 photos but a one-sentence description** — no options list, no condition report, mileage absent, spec chips inconsistent (the custom Peterbilts show nothing). "See All Specs" is effectively empty. A Flickr link offers 300+ more photos, unlabeled. [V — flagship detail page]
3. So he calls or texts **Dylan's cell**, or submits a form ("a sales representative will respond as soon as possible"). After 6pm, on a Sunday, or while Dylan is with another buyer: **the only capture is a generic web form.** No chat, no SMS widget, no callback booking, no after-hours anything. [V — absence confirmed across ~10 captured pages]
4. Photo/spec/video requests, budget, trade, financing all happen in calls and texts with Dylan. Six specific photos of the generator bay → Dylan walks the lot with his phone (he personally runs the 173K-photo Flickr operation [V]).
5. Showing is **by appointment only** — booked by phone; there is no online scheduler. [V]
6. Financing: a bare "Apply for Credit" form; no lender partners or terms on-site [V]. Certified funds; 10% deposit.
7. Post-sale: delivery nationwide — and this is where the negative reviews live (follow-through, deposit handling). [V]

**Seller/consignor journey (they actively buy):** RacingJunk carries a placeholder listing — *"ARE YOU SELLING? WE ARE BUYING! PLEASE CALL OR TEXT DYLAN"* [V — listing exists, priced $1,234,567,890]. They advertise BUY–SELL–TRADE–CONSIGN, but **the site has no consignment or sell-your-coach form at all** — seller intake is also Dylan's phone. [V — absence]

**The kicker from the field:** an iRV2 forum member reports the lot holds *"a huge number of super Cs… most not listed for sale anywhere."* [S — thread snippet] The online store undersells the physical inventory.

**Friction summary:** every step of a $500K remote transaction — inquiry, qualification, photo fulfillment, scheduling, financing, post-sale — funnels through one person's phone, with no capture net outside business hours and listings that force buyers to call for basics.

**The niche has no AI bar yet.** Every direct race-hauler/toterhome competitor checked runs on phone + email, business hours only [V/S — each site inspected]:

| Competitor | Distance/reach | Digital inbound | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Flying A Motorsports** (Scott City, MO) | **~1h45m away**, 3 states | Phone/email; no verified chat/24-7; Sat 9–12 | Nearest and most dangerous: Renegade + ShowHauler dealer, takes trades and consignments, financing dept |
| **Transwest** (25+ locations) | National | **Saved-search + price-drop alerts**, account system — but no chat/AI/24-7 | The most digitally sophisticated; the alert playbook to copy |
| Capitol Renegade (MD) | National | No chat/text surfaced | Preferred Renegade/ShowHauler dealer |
| Westside Motorcoach (IL) | Regional | Phone/email, M–F 9–4 | Publishes delivery at $1.75–$2.35/loaded mile |
| Elite Motorsports (OK) | National (NHRA ties) | Phone/form only | No financing/trade pages at all |

- **Presentation bar:** Motor Home Specialist (TX) puts video walkthroughs and posted prices on everything — and **Chase already matches the video bar** (403 walkthroughs, 34K subs). Their weakness is text/data, not media. [V]
- **The bar is moving underneath the niche:** RV TechAI launched July 2025 selling 24/7 AI voice + missed-call text-back to RV dealers at **$197–$597/mo**; RVDA is running AI workshops; Kenect and Podium market AI text/chat to RV & powersports dealers. None of Chase's direct competitors have adopted it yet. **First-mover window is open, and Flying A is the one most likely to close it.** [V/S — rv-pro.com, rvbusiness.com, kenect.com]

**Industry benchmarks (for slides — always labeled as benchmarks, never as Dylan's numbers):**
- Pied Piper 2026 Powersports Internet Lead Effectiveness study: industry average **44/100**, fifth straight stagnant year; only 47% of dealers answered a web lead's question; **9% of inquiries got no response at all**; only 13% offered an appointment time. [V — businesswire/Pied Piper]

- RVIA: 2025 closed at 342,220 wholesale shipments (+2.5%); 2026 forecast ~349K, best since 2022; premium Super C segment reportedly growing well above market rate [V — rvia.org; growth-rate claim is secondary-source, label if used].
